Apple Watch

Make one Shortcut for each button you want, on your iPhone:

  1. Shortcuts app → + → add the action Get Contents of URL.
  2. Paste a link from below into it (Copy, then tap the URL field).
  3. Add Show Result so the watch tells you what happened — it says if no screen is listening.
  4. Name it (e.g. “Focus”), then in its ⓘ Details turn on Show on Apple Watch.
  5. On the watch, add the Shortcuts complication to your face and choose it — one tap starts or stops the timer. On an Ultra, set the Action button to it.

Want everything in one shortcut? Use Choose from Menu with five items, each holding its own Get Contents of URL.
